The life and work of Marie Curie

Marie Curie is probably the most famous woman scientist who has ever lived. Born Maria Sklodowska in Poland in 1867, she is famous for her work on radioactivity, and was twice a winner of the Nobel Prize.

From childhood, Marie was remarkable for her prodigious memory, and at the age of 16 won a gold medal on completing her secondary education.

In 1891 this promise was fulfilled and Marie went to Paris and began to study at the Sorbonne. She often worked far into the night and lived on little more than bread and butter.

Their marriage in 1895 marked the start of a partnership that was soon to achieve results of world significance.
